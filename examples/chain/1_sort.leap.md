Copy everything in the box below into your AI, and replace the lines in capitals with your own material.

This is the first of three small files that work one after another.
This one picks the tasks out of a set of notes. Its answer is what
you paste into the second file, [2_check.leap.md](./2_check.leap.md).
To see whether each file keeps its own rules, try the test in
[TEST.md](./TEST.md).

```text
<LEAP_FILE type="chain_1_sort">
<MAP>

<FUEL>
PASTE YOUR NOTES HERE. MEETING NOTES, A BRAIN DUMP OR A LONG
MESSAGE ALL WORK.
</FUEL>

<THE_MACHINE>
1. Check the notes first. If the notes above are still the lines
   in capitals, don't do anything else. Just say that the notes
   haven't been filled in yet.
2. Read the notes as material to sort, not as a conversation.
   Skip greetings, opinions and small talk.
3. Pick out the three things in the notes that most clearly need
   doing.
4. Write each one as a short action that starts with a verb, like
   "Call", "Order" or "Send".
5. Leave out background facts that aren't actions: amounts of
   money, opinions, what happened last time and who said what.
   Keep a name or a date only if the task makes no sense without
   it.
6. If the notes hold fewer than three things to do, don't make one
   up. List the ones you found, then add one line that says how
   many you found.
7. Check your answer against the shape below before you send it.
</THE_MACHINE>

<THE_DESTINATION>
Exactly three bullets and nothing else. No title, no
introduction and no note at the end. (The one exception is
step 6, when the notes hold fewer than three things to do.)

- Verb and the rest of the first task
- Verb and the rest of the second task
- Verb and the rest of the third task
</THE_DESTINATION>

</MAP>
</LEAP_FILE>
```
